1. Talent Acquisition

Part A: Recruitment (Requisition → Approval → Hiring → Selection → Offer) · Part B: Onboarding (Form → Documents → Verification → Joining → Probation)
  • Part A – Recruitment: requisition, approval, hiring, interviews, selection or rejection, and the offer. Part B – Onboarding: starts when the candidate accepts the offer and runs through documents, joining and probation.
  • A requisition is created; if it is a replacement, the replaced employee is selected. The replaced employee's assets are returned through the Employee Exit module (separate) and go back to the asset pool for reallocation.
  • Approval: Line Manager → Cross Line Manager → HR. HR (higher management) decides who/how many approvers are needed, so the last step is a configurable multi-layer chain (Level 1…N).
  • Asset allocation (during recruitment): after approval HR only selects the device(s) for the new employee. This is a tentative selection; nothing is final yet.
  • Job posted on multiple channels; CVs collected and sorted by keywords; HR shortlists; multi-round interviews with marking.
  • Candidate rejection: a candidate not selected (at shortlisting or after any interview round) receives a rejection notice and is either closed or kept in a talent pool for future vacancies. The reason is recorded.
  • Offer stage: for the selected candidate HR prepares an offer (designation, salary from the salary structure, proposed joining date, probation period, validity date). The offer is approved (HR / higher management, configurable), sent as an offer letter, and the candidate accepts, negotiates or declines. A declined or expired offer goes to the next shortlisted candidate or reopens the requisition.
  • After the offer is accepted an onboarding form is sent to the employee. The employee fills it in and attaches all required documents by a due date.
  • HR reviews every document (verified / rejected with reason) and decides whether all info is collected. If anything is missing or wrong, it goes back to the employee; HR can only confirm once all info is collected.
  • Asset finalization: once all employee info is collected, HR can modify the asset info selected earlier, then confirm. The Employee record is created on confirmation.
  • Joining date and first-day tasks: HR confirms the joining date; on day one the employee gets an ID card, email and system access, receives the assets (handover signed), signs the policy acknowledgement (Annex B), and is set up with a roster, leave balances, attendance device and payroll details.
  • Probation: every new employee starts a probation (duration configurable, set in the offer). The Line Manager reviews at the end (and optionally mid-way). HR then confirms, extends or ends the probation; an ended probation moves to the Exit module.
  • No-show: if the candidate does not join on the joining date, the offer is cancelled, assets are released and the next candidate is considered.

2. Leave

Leave types, entitlement, application deadlines, holiday counting, compensatory leave
  • Employee applies according to leave type (each type has its own rules/balance).
  • Approval: Line Manager → Cross Line Manager → HR (confirmed). The chain is configurable per leave type; the policy document currently names only the Line Manager and HR, so the policy should be updated to include the Cross Line Manager.
  • HR can approve at any stage (access to all stages).
  • Per policy: apply via the HR system or the prescribed leave form (Annex A) to the Line Manager; if declined, the reason must be stated; HR records approved leave and updates the balance.
  • Leave year = calendar year (1 Jan – 31 Dec). Casual and Sick leave lapse on 31 Dec; no carry-forward and no encashment.
  • Management may reschedule leave, approve fewer days, cancel approved leave or call an employee back when the business requires.
  • Before leave the employee hands over urgent work to a colleague (Work Handed Over To and Contact Number During Leave are captured on the form).

System rules

  • Gender-based eligibility: Maternity for female employees only, Paternity for male employees only; Maternity usage count (max 2) and surviving-children check.
  • Sick leave overflow: after 14 days, extra sick days are adjusted first from Casual, then Earned, then become Leave Without Pay (Management discretion). A sick-leave extension not recommended by a doctor is treated as Earned leave or LWP.
  • Holidays during Casual / Sick leave: a weekly/festival holiday inside the leave period (between first and last day) is counted as leave; one just before or after it is not counted. Example: Thu–Sat with Friday between = 3 days; Thu only = 1 day; Sat only = 1 day.
  • Leave Without Pay applies when all entitled leave is used and extra leave is approved, or when absent without approved leave (may also lead to disciplinary action).
  • Document required (e.g. doctor's certificate for sick leave > 3 days) must be attached or the application cannot be completed.

Roster assignment

  • HR creates rosters and assigns a roster to each employee (or a group/department).
  • Every employee works according to their assigned roster: its start time decides On time / Late / Extreme Late, and its hours decide the working time.
  • Employees on an Open roster are exempt from late rules; only the 8 working hours are counted.

  • The grace period, Extreme Late limit and absent cut-off are configurable per roster. The defaults follow the policy, which has no grace period and treats arrival after 11:00 AM as absent.
  • Late and Extreme Late both count toward the monthly late-entry slab in Payroll (3 late entries = 1 day basic salary). Extreme Late adds visibility and escalation only, because the policy has no separate penalty.
  • Employees on an Open roster are exempt from late rules; only 8 working hours are counted.
  • Approved leave, approved WFH, approved outside-office duty or an HR-approved missing punch cancels a late or absent mark for that day.

Shift changes

  • Permanent change: HR reassigns the employee's roster with an effective date; the employee and Line Manager are notified.
  • Temporary change or swap: an employee (or the Line Manager) requests a different shift for specific dates, or a swap with a colleague. The colleague consents, the Line Manager approves, then HR approves.
  • Checks before approval: no clash with approved leave; the weekly holiday and alternate-Saturday schedule are kept; the roster of both employees stays valid after a swap; a maximum number of changes per month (configurable).
  • Roster changes are blocked for a payroll month that is locked; changes after that go through an HR adjustment.
  • Late and absent status always use the roster in force on that date.

4. Attendance

In Office or Work From Home
  • Four ways to give attendance: (1) In Office, (2) WFH, (3) Outside Office on approved duty, (4) Mobile attendance from a phone with location capture. WFH and Outside Office need approval first.
  • Office: captured from the Tip Soi and one ZKTeco device.
  • Mobile attendance: the employee marks check-in and check-out from the phone app. The app records time, GPS location, accuracy and device ID, and checks the place against the allowed area. It is allowed only when HR enables it for the employee, or for an approved WFH or outside-office duty.
  • WFH: multi-level approval – Line Manager → Cross Line Manager → HR → CEO/MD. HR can approve at all stages.
  • HR open date: HR sets and submits a date; on that date no WFH approval is needed – employees just give attendance.
  • Every employee, at any level, must punch in on entering and punch out on leaving using the attendance device. For outside visits, mark presence in the office first unless truly unavoidable.
  • Going directly from home on official duty: Line Manager approval in advance (with date(s)) and HR informed at the same time. If forgotten, submit with justification approved by the Functional Head within 48 hours; after that the day may be treated as absent.
  • Going out during office hours (personal or official): inform Line Manager and HR before leaving, punch out when leaving and punch in on return.
  • Leaving early: up to 3 days a month with prior Line Manager approval; without approval, or beyond 3 days, it may be treated as a late entry or half-day absence (Management discretion).
  • Forgot to punch: inform Line Manager and HR the same day; attendance corrected with approval (manual attendance correction request).
  • Late / absent: arrival after 9:30 AM is a late entry; after 11:00 AM is absent. Absence without approved leave is Leave Without Pay and may lead to disciplinary action.

Outside-office attendance (approval based)

  • An employee who must work outside the office (client visit, field work, direct-from-home official duty) first requests Outside-Office duty with the date(s), place or client, and purpose.
  • Approval: Line Manager → HR (HR can approve at any stage). Per policy the Line Manager approval must be in place before the duty; HR is informed at the same time.
  • If the request was forgotten, it can be submitted with a justification approved by the Functional Head within 48 hours; after that the day is treated as absent.
  • Once approved, the employee gives attendance from the phone (Mobile attendance below), and the approved place is used as the allowed location.

WFH location / IP check

  • After WFH is approved, attendance given from the web or phone is checked against the employee's registered home location (GPS radius) and/or allowed IP range (for example home or VPN).
  • If the check passes the attendance is recorded; if it fails it is flagged for HR, who accepts it (with a note) or marks the day absent.
  • On an HR open date the approval is skipped but the location / IP check still applies, so attendance is not accepted from an unknown place without a flag.

Manual attendance (missing punch)

  • Cases: forgot to punch, device or app failure, missing check-out.
  • The employee submits a manual attendance request (date, time, reason, proof if any) and informs the Line Manager. Per policy this is done the same day; later requests need an explicit HR decision.
  • HR approves or rejects every missing-punch request. HR can also enter manual attendance on behalf of an employee with a reason.
  • When approved the day's status is recalculated (late, early leave, hours); a rejected request leaves the day as recorded.
  • Every manual entry keeps who requested, who approved, the old value and the new value (audit trail), and a monthly count per employee is shown in the reports.

5. Payroll

Salary structure, deductions, statutory items, payment, corrections, final settlement
  • Payroll is processed monthly from attendance, leave, holiday and WFH data. Late entries are counted fresh each month and never carry over.
  • Basic salary = the basic component of monthly salary, excluding allowances. Late deductions are calculated on basic salary only.
  • All deduction slabs and amounts are configurable by HR; the values below are the current policy defaults.
  • Every deduction line on the payslip shows its reason and source (e.g. "4 late entries in Oct").

4. Calculation rules

  • Monthly cut-off: attendance and leave are locked for the month before payroll runs; later corrections (forgotten punch, late-approved leave within the 3-working-day window) go through an HR adjustment so the month can be reprocessed.
  • Order of processing: apply approved leave first, then absence / LWP, then late slabs, then early-leave penalties, then manual deductions.
  • Approved leave and approved WFH / official duty are never counted as late or absent.
  • Open-roster employees are exempt from late rules; only the 8 working hours are counted.
  • Operational units (ISP, Food, Field) use their own roster; the same deduction rules apply against that roster.
  • Daily rate: "1 day of salary" is not defined in the policy. It is a configuration setting: monthly basic ÷ 30 (proposed default) or ÷ working days of the month.
  • Salary components, statutory items and other deductions follow the Salary structure and Statutory sections below; every rate is configurable and effective-dated.

  • Probation salary (if different) and the confirmed salary are both stored; the salary changes on the probation confirmation date.
  • Revisions (increment, promotion, correction) are new effective-dated entries with an approval (HR → higher management) and a full history; old payroll months never change.
  • Bank or mobile-banking details are collected during onboarding and verified by HR; a change needs HR approval.
  • Partial months (joining mid-month, leaving mid-month, unpaid leave) are pro-rated using the daily-rate setting.

8. Corrections

  • Arrears: an underpayment found later (late approved leave, wrong rate, missed increment) is added to the next payroll as a separate arrear line with the month it belongs to.
  • Overpayment recovery: an excess payment is recovered as a deduction in the next payroll, in one go or instalments approved by HR.
  • Revised payroll: default is an adjustment line in the next month. A locked month is reopened only with HR and approver permission, and only if payment has not been made or has been reversed.
  • Payslip regeneration: a corrected payslip is issued as a new version (v2, v3) and the old one stays in history, marked superseded.
  • Every correction records who changed what, the old and new value, the reason and the approver (audit trail), and shows on the monthly correction report.

9. Final settlement

  • Trigger: the Employee Exit module (separate) sends the last working day, the clearance status and the asset recovery amount. Payroll only calculates and pays the settlement.
  • Earnings: salary up to the last working day (pro-rated), pending allowances and arrears, pro-rata festival bonus if the rule allows, leave encashment only where the policy allows (Casual and Sick are never encashed; Earned leave is to be confirmed), PF and gratuity payout where applicable.
  • Deductions: late / absent / LWP for the last month, unrecovered advances or loans, asset recovery for unreturned or damaged items, notice-period shortfall if the contract has one, and income tax.
  • Gate: settlement is prepared only after the Exit module marks clearance (handover and asset return) as complete, and HR can override with a note.
  • Approval: HR → Accounts / higher management; then paid through the normal payment run or a separate payment; a settlement statement is given to the employee.

Open questions

  • Late rule: defaults follow the policy (no grace, Extreme Late after 30 minutes, absent after 11:00). Confirm the grace period (if any), the Extreme Late limit, and whether Extreme Late should carry a heavier deduction than a normal late entry.
  • Late deduction for 12 or more late entries: the policy stops at 9 – 11. Default is +1 day per 3 late entries.
  • Earned (Privilege) leave: the policy lists it but gives no entitlement, accrual, pay or encashment rule.
  • Policy update: the policy names only the Line Manager for leave approval, while this document uses Line Manager → Cross Line Manager → HR. The policy also has section-numbering gaps (Earned Leave is in the contents but missing in the body) and does not cover WFH, mobile attendance or location tracking.
  • Offer and probation: probation length (for example 3 or 6 months), who approves an offer, offer validity period, and whether leave and PF rules differ during probation.
  • Documents: confirm the proposed checklist and which documents are mandatory for which designation.
  • Mobile attendance and tracking: tracking interval, geofence radius, whether a selfie is required, and legal / consent wording for location tracking.
  • WFH check: whether a home location, an IP range, or both will be used, and how employees register them.
  • Payroll: formula for one day of salary, salary components and allowance rules, who approves the payroll run, payment date, and the bank / mobile-banking file formats Accounts needs.
  • Statutory: income tax slabs and rebate, provident fund scheme, festival bonus rules, gratuity, and overtime (not in the policy).
  • Final settlement: leave encashment (Earned leave), notice-period shortfall, and who approves it.
